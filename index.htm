Baking calculator FAQs

How do I calculate the cost of making a cake?

Add the cost of ingredients you use (price per kg, litre or piece × amount used), plus packaging, labour, energy, overheads and wastage. Divide by the units made.

How does the selling price calculation work?

With Target Profit Margin selected, the formula is: Selling price = Cost per unit ÷ (1 − Margin% − Selling Fee%). This guarantees that after fees and costs are paid, you retain your target percentage.

What is the difference between profit margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price. Markup is profit as a share of your cost. For example, a ₹400 profit on a ₹600 cost is a 40% margin and a 66.7% markup.

How do I scale a cake recipe for a different pan size?

Enter the dimensions of your original and target tins in the tin converter. Click "Use tin factor in scaling" to automatically adjust your recipe ingredients.
